Palm92 AI Governance Check

Could your AI workflow survive scrutiny?

Answer Yes, Partly or No. Your result is calculated in your browser.

1. Purpose

Is the AI system's purpose and decision boundary clear?

2. Evidence

Can you trace the evidence used to reach an output?

3. Identity

Can you identify which person or agent performed an action?

4. Permissions

Are access and permissions limited and reviewable?

5. Human approval

Are consequential actions held for accountable human approval?

6. Audit trail

Are important actions and changes logged?

7. Uncertainty

Can uncertainty, exceptions and overrides be recorded?

8. Privacy & security

Are sensitive data and security risks considered?

9. Escalation

Is there a clear path when something goes wrong?

10. Accountability

Is a named human or team accountable for the outcome?

This diagnostic is an educational governance aid, not legal, regulatory or compliance advice.
