## Choose a workflow

### Solar Enquiry

Prepare a first response to a residential solar enquiry for an installer in Lagos or Abuja.

**Example request**

I live in London. My mum in Lekki needs a fridge and two fans for six hours.

**From the draft**

Thanks for explaining what your mum needs in Lekki. Could you share the fridge model and let us know if she already has an inverter or batteries? That will help us prepare for an assessment.

### Book Promo

Create a short sales script and shot list for an independent author using their own book description.

**Example request**

Fictional title: Sunday at Number Nine. A daughter returns to Lagos to reopen her mother’s bookshop. Readers can message the author to order.

**From the draft**

Opening line: “What brings a daughter back to the bookshop her mother left behind?” Film the cover, introduce the daughter’s decision, then invite readers to message the author to order.

### Supplier Evidence

Turn a supplier questionnaire or evidence excerpt into a review table for a small procurement team.

**Example request**

Vendor says backups run nightly. An undated policy says weekly. No restore record supplied.

**From the draft**

Backups | Partial and conflicting | Supplier says nightly, policy says weekly | Ask the supplier for the current schedule and a dated restore-test record.

### Quote Follow-up

Turn a quote and customer conversation into an approval-ready follow-up for a small service business.

**Example request**

A customer asked for an itemised quote on Tuesday and wants to discuss labour. Draft a follow-up.

**From the draft**

Thanks for reviewing the quote. Would it help if I walked you through the labour line before you decide?

### Transcript Clips

Create a source-grounded clip plan for a creator who owns or can reuse a talk or recording.

**Example request**

My own talk: “A quote is not the first step. First ask which appliances must stay on when the power goes.” No timestamps.

**From the draft**

Clip opening: “A quote is not the first step.” End after “when the power goes.” Caption: Which appliances matter most during a power cut?

### Pilot Offer

Turn a niche workflow into a one-page pilot offer and demand test for an independent consultant.

**Example request**

Independent authors have a cover and synopsis but struggle to record their first promo.

**From the draft**

Pilot: Turn one synopsis into a 30-second spoken script and three phone-camera shots. Ask the author whether they recorded it and whether they would buy a second script.
